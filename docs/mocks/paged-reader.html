Read on e-ink. Talk it over on your phone.

KOReader MCP is a plugin that connects your e-reader to the AI app you already use.

Turn the page to see how.

You ask from your phone

  • What does the word I selected mean?
  •  Checked the page on your reader 
  • You selected “hypos”, in the first chapter of Moby-Dick. It’s 19th-century slang for a fit of low spirits.
  • Highlight that sentence and add a note.
  •  Saved a highlight and a note in the book 

The assistant sees the page you’re on, searches the book, and saves highlights and notes into it.

Three steps to set up

  1. Install the plugin from KOReader’s App Store plugin, or copy the folder onto your reader.
  2. Turn on the MCP server in the Tools menu.
  3. Add it to your AI app as a connector. Claude and Gemini are tested.
